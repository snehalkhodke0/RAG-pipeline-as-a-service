# World Geography Facts

## Mountains

Mount Everest, located in the Himalayas on the border between Nepal and the Tibet
Autonomous Region of China, is the tallest mountain above sea level at 8,849 meters.
K2, the second highest mountain in the world at 8,611 meters, is located in the
Karakoram range on the border between Pakistan and China.

## Rivers

The Nile River, flowing through northeastern Africa, is traditionally considered the
longest river in the world at approximately 6,650 kilometers. The Amazon River in
South America carries by far the largest discharge of water of any river, and some
measurements suggest it may rival or exceed the Nile in total length.

## Oceans

The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest of the world's oceans, covering more
area than all of Earth's land combined. The Mariana Trench, located in the western
Pacific, contains the deepest known point in Earth's oceans, the Challenger Deep, at
approximately 10,935 meters below sea level.

## Deserts

The Sahara Desert in North Africa is the largest hot desert in the world, covering
about 9.2 million square kilometers. Antarctica, despite its ice, is technically
classified as the largest desert overall because desert is defined by low
precipitation rather than temperature.

## Capital Cities

Tokyo is the capital of Japan and the most populous metropolitan area in the world.
Canberra, not Sydney, is the capital of Australia, a common point of confusion. Ottawa
is the capital of Canada, while Brasilia, a planned city built in the 1950s, is the
capital of Brazil.

## Lakes

Lake Baikal in Siberia, Russia, is both the deepest and the oldest freshwater lake in
the world, holding roughly 20 percent of the world's unfrozen fresh water. The
Caspian Sea, bordered by five countries, is the largest inland body of water on
Earth, though its salinity means it is often classified as a sea rather than a lake.

## Countries

Russia is the largest country in the world by land area, spanning eleven time zones.
Vatican City is the smallest sovereign state in the world, both by area and
population, located entirely within the city of Rome, Italy.
